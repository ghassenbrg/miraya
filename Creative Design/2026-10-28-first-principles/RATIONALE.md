# Rationale — proposal only

## Three directions

1. **Reflection Seam — selected.** Two mirrored facets create an ownable silhouette and a central state seam. It works equally well for first pairing, repeat connection, live sharing and pause/stop, and survives small icon sizes without motion.
2. **Relay Ribbon.** A continuous relay ribbon creates strong motion language, but it becomes less distinctive at 16–32 px and pushes a calm utility toward a timeline metaphor.
3. **Trust Aperture.** Six points around a core make pairing memorable, but the identity over-indexes on first-run verification instead of the much more frequent reconnect/share task.

## Why Reflection Seam

It connects identity and interaction without decorative illustration. On Mac the user sees one relationship—source to destination—with essential share controls primary and technical controls disclosed later. On TV the same seam is secondary to large state copy and remote actions.

No live desktop preview is included or implied. The design uses source metadata rather than a fake preview.
