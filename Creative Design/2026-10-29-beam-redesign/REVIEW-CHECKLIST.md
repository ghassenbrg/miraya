# Design review checklist

**PROPOSAL ONLY — NOT IMPLEMENTED**

Use the prototype and PNGs side by side with the approved Beam reference.

- [ ] Large icon light, material depth and rim finish meet the reference’s quality bar.
- [ ] Sender and Receiver feel related but distinguishable; names and micro shape cues remain clear.
- [ ] The Beam is recognizable at 16 / 32 px without becoming a cast, Wi-Fi, play or VPN icon.
- [ ] Light/dark, mono, horizontal and vertical lockups work; outlined wordmarks render without fonts.
- [ ] Mac ready makes Choose TV → Choose source → Share obvious at 340 / 420 / 540 pt.
- [ ] Share acknowledges immediately; waiting, comparison, approval and first picture are distinct.
- [ ] Both endpoints show a six-digit comparison, never long-code entry; TV approval defaults Decline.
- [ ] Pause / Resume differs from Stop; Stop stays visible; TV Back behavior is clear.
- [ ] Audio says system audio, never microphone; Mac / TV / Both and TV playback gain are understandable.
- [ ] Quality is explicitly a ceiling; effective output is visible and metrics are labelled Illustrative.
- [ ] Fit / Fill / Stretch and symmetric Edge Margin communicate bars, crop and distortion accurately.
- [ ] App viewport, physical output and Miraya ceiling cannot be mistaken for one another.
- [ ] Discovery and permission recovery give useful next actions without inventing a diagnosis.
- [ ] Trust can be removed one device at a time, including an offline device, with confirmation.
- [ ] The mountain/lake backdrop, amber plane and water reflection faithfully match the approved reference.
- [ ] Pairing glass and text remain readable against the artwork; live wallpaper is clearly illustrative.
- [ ] TV focus and pairing digits remain legible from the couch; physical validation is still outstanding.
- [ ] TV live content is primary; invoked controls are compact and navigable by D-pad.
- [ ] Future iOS is labelled; TV-side mirror pause, live previews and Netflix handoff are not presented as implemented.
- [ ] Review the [validation record](VALIDATION.md) and its limitations before approving implementation.

Approval should specify whether the identity, icons, Mac experience and TV experience are accepted together or need targeted refinement. No approval or production change has been assumed.
